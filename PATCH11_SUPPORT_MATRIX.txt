PROPRIETARY / LICENSED VARIANTS
-------------------------------
SQRC                            PARTIAL: public QR data can be read; private
                                data requires the issuer cryptographic key and
                                DENSO-compatible reader.
DENSO FrameQR                   NOT natively decoded by the open-source engine.
                                Full support requires DENSO decoder/Q Platform
                                or another licensed compatible implementation.

NOTE
----
'SQRC' and 'FrameQR' are not merely extra names that can be enabled in a generic
QR library. They have proprietary reading/security requirements, so Patch 11
does not falsely claim full decoding of protected/proprietary content.
