PRS.AssetVerify V2 Patch 12 — Barcode Support
==============================================

BARCODE-FIRST / PRIMARY TARGETS
- Code 128
- GS1-128 / UCC-EAN-128
- Code 39
- Code 93
- UPC-A
- UPC-E
- EAN-13
- EAN-8
- ITF / Interleaved 2 of 5
- ITF-14
- Standard / Industrial 2 of 5 (fallback)
- Codabar
- GS1 DataBar family (ZXing-C++)
- MSI / Modified Plessey (fallback)
- Code 32 (Quagga2)
- Pharmacode (Quagga2 / JS fallback)
- Telepen and additional ZXing-C++ linear formats may also decode through AllLinear.

QR / MATRIX
Existing QR / matrix capability is preserved as a final compatibility fallback,
but Patch 12 is intentionally tuned for 1D barcodes first.

CAPTURE GUIDANCE
1D barcodes need their "quiet zones" — blank space immediately to the left and
right of the bars. The app now preserves these areas during preprocessing, but the
camera cannot reconstruct margins that were cut out of the original photo.
For best results:
- keep the whole barcode in the photo;
- include blank space on BOTH sides;
- let the bars occupy most of the image width;
- avoid glare crossing the bars;
- hold the phone parallel to the label;
- tap the barcode area to focus before taking the photo when iOS allows it.
