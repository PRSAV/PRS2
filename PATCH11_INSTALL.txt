PRS.AssetVerify V2 — Patch 11 Universal Barcode Engine
=====================================================
Baseline: V2 Patch 10 (iPhone Native Scanner)

Purpose
-------
Keep the working Patch 10 iPhone native-camera workflow unchanged, but replace
its decoder layer with a broader multi-engine reader for 1D/2D symbols.

Deploy
------
Replace ONLY these three root files in PRSAV/PRS2:
  1. app.js
  2. index.html
  3. sw.js

Do not replace styles.css, manifest.webmanifest, icon.svg, Worker code, database,
or any existing data.

After merge into main:
  1. Wait for GitHub Pages deployment.
  2. Close all PRS Safari tabs on iPhone.
  3. Reopen the site.
  4. Open Scan & Verify.
  5. Confirm the status says: "Universal scanner ready (V2 Patch 11)."

Decoder architecture
--------------------
Primary: zxing-wasm 3.1.3 / ZXing-C++ WebAssembly (AllReadable)
Fallback: native BarcodeDetector where available, then ZBar polyfill
MSI fallback: javascript-barcode-reader 1.0.0

The decoder dependencies are loaded from fixed-version jsDelivr/unpkg URLs and
cached by the existing service worker after successful use. The iPhone native
camera itself remains independent of these decoder downloads.

Important proprietary-code limitation
--------------------------------------
SQRC: ordinary QR readers can read the public portion. Private SQRC data requires
the relevant cryptographic key and a DENSO-compatible/dedicated reader.
FrameQR: DENSO FrameQR is proprietary and ordinary/open-source QR decoders do not
provide full FrameQR decoding. Full FrameQR support requires DENSO's decoder/Q
Platform or another licensed compatible implementation.

Rollback
--------
Restore Patch 10 app.js, index.html and sw.js.
